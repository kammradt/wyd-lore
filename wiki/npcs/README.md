# NPCs

*NPCs.* Personagens não jogáveis e o que oferecem.

| Página | English | Resumo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [GodMaster](godmaster.md) | GodMaster | The GodMaster NPC in Armia (X:2126 Y:2123) trades class-specific skill books such as the 10th and 11th skills; books are account-bound and purchases cannot be undone. |
| [DivineMerchant (Victory Coupom)](divine-merchant.md) | DivineMerchant (Victory Coupon) | The DivineMerchant NPC in Armia (X:2143 Y:2087) sells account-bound items for Victory Coupons dropped in LAN N, M and A, including Pedra da Imortalidade for Arch creation, mount Amagos and the Pedra Ideal (Limitada) for Celestial creation. |
| [9th Master](9th-master.md) | 9th Master | The 9th Master NPC in Armia (X:2136 Y:2115) provides the ninth skill book for each class; books are account-bound and purchases cannot be undone. |
| [Jazeg](jazeg.md) | Jazeg | The Jazeg NPC (2117 2081) changes the bonus option on Amuleto Arcano (HP) and Amuleto Arcano (MP) items refined to +11 or +15, with no chance of failure. |
| [Dedekinto: Criação de Lac 100](dedekinto.md) | Dedekinto: Lac 100 Crafting | The Dedekinto NPC composes an account-bound Poeira de Lac 100 from 10 Poeira de Lac and 1 Shiny Laktorerium pieces; all materials are destroyed on failure. |

# Sistemas

*Game systems.* Mecânicas do jogo: refinação, guildas, reinos, montarias e mais.

| Página | English | Resumo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Interface](interface.md) | Interface | Explains the main elements of the WYD game screen, including the HP, MP and mount HP bars, mini map, chat window, potion counter and experience bar, along with their keyboard shortcuts. |
| [Controles](controls.md) | Controls | Covers basic game controls: mouse movement and attacks, using skills from the quick slots (1-10 / F1-F10), toggling auto-attack with the T key, and buying from merchant NPCs and the storage. |
| [Grupo](party.md) | Party | Describes how to form, join and leave a party and kick members, and the experience bonus table that grows from 180% for 2 members to 300% for 8 or more. |
| [Modo C.C. (Controle de Combate/Macro)](combat-control-macro.md) | C.C. Mode (Combat Control/Macro) | Explains C.C. Mode, the auto-combat macro that attacks or casts skills on monsters in range, uses buffs from the first quick slot, and drinks potions or feeds the mount automatically at set HP percentages; it never attacks other players. |
| [Replation](replation.md) | Replation | Covers the Pedra Replation, sold in classes A to C by the merchant Lucy in Azran, which changes the class of armor at +3 or lower and adds a random class attribute on success but destroys the item on failure. |
| [Sistema de Reinos](kingdoms.md) | Kingdom System | Explains the lore of the split into the kingdoms Hekalotia and Akelonia, how to join one from level 220 by paying the King in Safira, leaving a kingdom for 16 Safiras, and the capes and PK experience benefits of membership. |
| [Sistema de Pintura](painting.md) | Painting System | Explains how to dye defensive gear (not weapons or accessories) by refining Tintura with Poeira de Oriharucon into Feijão Mágico, bought from Premium Neil; only items at +1 or higher can be painted. |
| [Sistema de Refinação](refinement.md) | Refinement System | Covers item refinement from +0 to +11 using Poeira de Oriharucon and Lactorerium, the Ancient weapon path, +10 composition at NPC Alieen in Azran with jewel effect gems, and +12 to +15 composition at Alquimista Odin in Kefra city with fixed success rates of 4% to 1%. |
| [Montaria](mounts.md) | Mounts | Explains how mounts grow from egg to foal to mount, how Amagos raise mount level up to 120, the bonuses mounts give (speed, damage, evasion, absorption) and the need to feed them; Grifo Sangrento eggs are for Celestial characters only. |
| [Evasão](evasion.md) | Evasion | Short guide on the evasion attribute, which gives enemy attacks a chance to miss, and how to raise it through dexterity and certain mounts. |
| [Ataque Crítico X4](critical-attack-x4.md) | Critical Attack x4 | Explains how attack speed lets physical characters multiply critical hits: 200% enables x2, 400% enables x3 and 800% enables x4 criticals, with example builds and their multiplier chances. |
| [Chaos Points](chaos-points.md) | Chaos Points | Describes the Chaos Points (CP) PK system ranging from -75 to +75, how killing players lowers CP, how leveling and the Pergaminho do Perdão raise it, the /cp command, and exceptions such as guild wars and city PK areas. |
| [Guild](guild.md) | Guild | Covers creating a guild for 100 million gold via the O panel (up to 52 members and 3 sub-leaders), member management, Fame Guild earned from Tower, City and Noatun wars and bosses like Kefra and SandLich, Guild Influence and the Guild Shop. |

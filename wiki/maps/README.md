# Mapas e dungeons

*Maps and dungeons.* Regiões, cidades e dungeons de Kersef.

| Página | English | Resumo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Armia](armia.md) | Armia | Map guide to Armia, the walled starting city of every new WYD character, listing its NPCs such as Martin, Galford, the Golden Dragon, Premium Neil, the Cargo Guard and the class skill masters. It also covers the guild zone, PvP zone, teleports to Noatun and the dungeons, and the training field. |
| [Nippleheim](nippleheim.md) | Nippleheim | Map and lore of Nippleheim, the frozen land north of Azran where Tzfah and his right hand Neses were exiled after the Celestial War, and where the Karden Command base was built to fight the dark elves. It lists local NPCs including July, Shany, Tiny, Martin and Premium Neil. |
| [Submundo](underworld.md) | Underworld | Lore of the Underworld, born from the war between the kingdoms of Hekalotia and Akelonia over the celestial ore 'Temuh'. The area is split into the Nikirr Dungeon (first level) and the Underworld (second level) beyond the River of Death, with demon-type monsters growing stronger deeper in. |
| [Dungeon Kefra](kefra-dungeon.md) | Kefra Dungeon | Covers the Kefra Dungeon map, its lore about the struggle over the Crown of Athila and the Kult Desert, and the weekly Kefra boss fight in the Kefra Hall, reached by giving a Sealed Scroll to the Survivor NPC. Defeating Kefra opens a portal on the beginner channel to the Unknown Continent, home of Alquimista Odin and Uxmal; drops include the Stone of Immortality and Sephirots. |
| [Dragon Field](dragon-field.md) | Dragon Field | Map reached through the Gold Dragon NPC in Azran with the Dragon Guardian item equipped (made from a Dragon Soul at the Dragon Elder). Part 1 is guarded by the boss Legend Verid, which drops the Mark of Archangel, and Part 2 by the bosses Nesses and Haden, which can unlock level 360 for celestial characters. |
| [Deserto Desconhecido](unknown-desert.md) | Unknown Desert | Map accessed through the NPC Angela Sister in Noatun on the beginner channel by handing in an Ancestral Artifact; it resets every 20 minutes. It drops Shiny Laktorerium pieces and the Dragon Soul used to reach Dragon Field, with monsters such as BoneDragon, Scorpion and Fire Gagoil. |
| [Zona Infernal](infernal-zone.md) | Infernal Zone | Daily group quest held at 22:00 and 22:30 for up to 52 players, entered with the Gate of Hell ticket and played in six phases of bosses and Lichs. It ends with protecting the NPC Dionys for a large experience reward; HellLich and ArchLich drop items such as Infinity Symbols, Moonstone and Jewel of Darkness. |
| [Pesadelo A](nightmare.md) | Nightmare A | Group leveling quest for high-level Arch and celestial characters, set in Azran, where the party protects 8 city NPCs for 15 minutes. Entry is possible at XX:10, XX:30 and XX:50, and the enemies include Taron, Iron Taron, Hell Spider, Manticora and Lugefer. |
| [Pista de Runas](rune-trail.md) | Rune Trail | Group quest crafted from seven Lactolerium dusts at Alquimista Odin and registered with the NPC Uxmal in the Kefra Hall, giving runes, drops, experience and the blessed refinement. It has seven stages from +0 to +6 (Lich, Tower, Amon, Wolf, Labyrinth, Barlog, Rabbit), each with its own goal and rune rewards. |
| [Coliseu](coliseum.md) | Coliseum | Daily PvP drop quest in the arena east of Azran on beginner servers, entered with the Public Notification item. It runs in three tiers at 19:00, 20:00 and 21:00, the first two only for mortals and Archs, and drops crystals, remains and Ori and Lac dusts. |

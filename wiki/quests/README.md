# Quests

*Quests.* Quests por faixa de level, com etapas, NPCs e recompensas.

| Página | English | Resumo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Campo de Treinamento](training-camp.md) | Training Camp | Beginner quest for levels 1-35 at the training camp east of Armia, split into four areas whose bosses (GremlinDecoy, GiantCrill, OrcSniper, OrcWarrior) drop keys to the next gate. Trainer NPCs (FirstTrainer, SecondTrainer, ThirdTrainer, TrainerChief) reward healing potions, random weapon options, refinement up to +6 and an Olho Crescente experience item, once per character. |
| [Defensor da Alma](spirit-guardian.md) | Spirit Guardian | Repeatable leveling quest for levels 40-115 in the Armia cemetery, entered via the NPC Gravekeeper, where players fight monsters said to be trying to revive the General da Morte. Monsters drop the Caixa da Sabedoria, which grants 30,000 experience when used. |
| [Conquista do Castelo Orc](conquering-orc-castle.md) | Conquering Orc Castle | Quest for levels 1-400 at the Orc Castle north of Armia, progressing through gates by defeating OrcKeeper, OrcGuard and CheifOrc for keys before facing the OrcLord. The reward is the Pílula do Poder Mágico, usable once per character for 9 skill points. |
| [Defensor da Terra Mística](defending-mystic-land.md) | Defending Mystic Land | One-time quest for levels 60-400 started with the NPC KnightsLeader in Azran, in which players defend the NPC ExploitLeader from monsters in the Mystic Lands south of Azran. The reward is a Silver, Gold or Mystic amulet depending on performance. |
| [Benção de Deus](bless-of-the-god.md) | Bless of the God | One-time quest for levels 70-75 north of Erion, started with the Zesty Priest, in which players hunt the NainTrollChief boss for the Pedaço da Chance relic and bring it to the priest Kruno. Kruno turns it into a Composto de Chance, which resets 50 points of skills and learning and cannot be used by Arch characters. |
| [Formatura de Cavaleiros Aprendizes](ceremony-to-apprentice.md) | Ceremony to Apprentice | One-time quest for levels 101-150 at the Knights' Training Field south of Azran, started with the NPC TS Chief. Players defeat TaursKing for the Emblema da Aprendizagem, then present it to the TS Leader to receive the Manto do Aprendiz cape. |
| [Jardim de Deus](gods-garden.md) | God's Garden | Repeatable leveling quest for levels 116-190 in God's Garden south of Azran, entered via the NPC Gardener. Monsters there drop the Lágrima Angelical, which grants 120,000 experience when used. |
| [Equilíbrio da Força](balance-of-power.md) | Balance of Power | One-time quest for levels 120-125 south of Azran tied to the Achro cathedral and the priestess Amelia, who keeps the balance between Akelonia and Hekalotia. Players defeat InionTrollChief for the Pedaço de Equilíbrio and receive the Composto de Equilíbrio, which resets 100 skill learning points. |
| [Ressurreição do Cavaleiro Negro](revival-of-the-dark-knight.md) | Revival of the Dark Knight | Repeatable leveling quest for levels 191-265 on the first floor of the Dungeon, where Kaizen's tomb has been desecrated and Kaizen revived by the Esqueleto Consery. Entered via the NPC Scout, it rewards the Coração de Kaizen, worth 250,000 experience. |
| [Molar do Gárgula](gargoyles-molar-tooth.md) | Gargoyle's Molar Tooth | Quest for levels 201-255 on the first floor of the Dungeon, accessed via the NPC Scout, where players hunt GagoilChief for the rare Molar do Gárgula. Using the single-use item refines equipped armor to +6. |
| [Cavaleiro do Reino](knight-of-the-kingdom.md) | Knight of the Kingdom | One-time quest for levels 220-250 in the center of the kingdoms, started with a RoyalGuard, in which players defeat DarkKnightKing for the Emblema do Reino. Swearing loyalty to KingGlantuar (Red) or KingHarabard (Blue) grants the Manto Akelonia or Manto Hekalotia respectively. |
| [Hidra Imortal](immortality-of-hydra.md) | Immortality of Hydra | Repeatable leveling quest for levels 266-320 on the first floor of the Dungeon, where Hydras were corrupted by the Olho de Sangue and bound to the ancient evil of Haden. Entered via the NPC Scout, it rewards the Olho de Sangue, worth 500,000 experience. |
| [Início da Infelicidade](beginning-of-unhappiness.md) | Beginning of Unhappiness | Repeatable leveling quest for levels 321-355 on the first floor of the Submundo, tied to the exiled Elves turned Dark Elves by Haden's power. Entered via the NPC Guard, it rewards the Pedra Espiritual dos Elfos, worth 800,000 experience. |
| [Desbloqueio de Capa 355](mantle-unlock-355.md) | Mantle Unlock 355 | Quest for levels 355-400 completed by using the Selo do Guerreiro, obtained from other players or the NPC Farche in Erion. Using the seal transforms the player's cape into the Elite de Akelonia (Red) or Elite de Hekalotia (Blue). |
| [À Procura dos Rastros do Mundo Celestial](traces-of-the-celestial-world.md) | Searching for Traces of the Celestial World | Special quest about seven crystals left over from the Celestial War, dropped by monsters around Armia. Bringing all seven (Cristal I to VII) to the Golden Dragon (Dragão Dourado) in Armia applies a random new attribute to a non-Arch weapon refined +10 or higher, or an Ancient weapon. |
| [Contrato dos Espíritos](arch-spirit-contract.md) | Spirit Contract | Four-phase one-time quest for Arch characters at level 355 with over 1,950,000,000 experience, using items hunted in the Kefra Dungeon. Each completed phase costs 100 million experience and raises ability values. |
| [Rainha Valquíria](valkyrie-queen.md) | Valkyrie Queen | Celestial-only, party-only quest in four stages with a 15-minute timer, starting by killing the IceQueen Mirror in Nipleheim and continuing in the Ice Castle against summoned monsters and the Sombra Negra. The final boss, Ice Queen Verid, always drops the Coração do Beriel plus one extra reward such as Alma do Beriel or Refinação Abençoada +1. |
| [Caçador de Espólios](spoils-hunter.md) | Spoils Hunter | Daily hunting quest requiring the Mercenary Coupon and a Normal or Mystic Hunter Costume, with time-based hand-ins to the NPCs HuntMaster/LootMaster or Huntsman/Lootsman. Rewards are Rubies, spent at the NPC RubiMaster, and Tesouro Perdido chests opened at the PirateMaster. |
| [Irmãos Nórdicos](nordic-brothers.md) | Nordic Brothers | Covers the artisan brothers Hoder, who adds an extra attribute to Bahamut and Amunra Cytheras, and Balder, who attempts refinement of Celestial characters' capes from +9 up to +13. Both NPCs are found inside the Zona Infernal quest map. |

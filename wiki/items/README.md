# Itens

*Items.* Armas, armaduras, acessórios e materiais.

| Página | English | Resumo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Armas Ancient](ancient-weapons.md) | Ancient Weapons | Explains how to combine a +9 weapon with a Diamond, Emerald, Coral or Garnet gem at the Compositor NPC in Azran to create an Ancient weapon with an extra attribute. Optional +7 to +10 items raise the success chance, and all items are lost on failure. |
| [Passando Adicional para Arma Arch](arch-weapon-additional-transfer.md) | Transferring Additional Stats to an Arch Weapon | Short guide to transferring an additional (add) to an Arch weapon at the NPC Tainy in Karden, using the target +9 Ancient weapon, a +9 Ancient weapon with the desired add, a +9 [D] or [E] item and 100,000,000 gold. The process can fail. |
| [Barra de Mithril](mithril-bar.md) | Mithril Bar | Guide to Mithril Bars, which improve the additional stats of Arch and celestial weapons and armor refined to at least +11, in Magic Attack, Damage, Critical, Defense and Immunity classes. It lists the maximum add per class and equipment slot and the threshold below which bars no longer reduce the add. |
| [Criação e Refinação de Armaduras Celestiais](celestial-armor.md) | Creating and Refining Celestial Armor | Guide to making celestial weapons and armor: sealed items are composed at InfWeapon and InfArmor near the Azran dragon, refined to +9 with Lac dust, then combined at Odin in the lost city of Kefra with a +15 Arch piece, a Moonstone and secret stones. It also covers the use of the Moonstone and Jewel of Darkness. |
| [Composição de Cintos](belts.md) | Belt Composition | Covers composing tier 1 and tier 2 belts at the NPC MystWizard using Symbols of Wisdom, Royal Coupons and, for tier 2, two matching tier 1 belts. The composition cannot fail and the resulting belts are untradeable. |
| [Mount Growth Stabilizer](mount-growth-stabilizer.md) | Mount Growth Stabilizer | Item used to refine mount cores and ease mount improvement, composed in packs of 100 at the NPC Conjurer Ehre in Erion from 5 Magic Seals, 100 Lac dusts and 1kk gold. The composition never fails. |
| [Pedra Espiritual E e F / Pedra Amunra](spirit-stones.md) | Spirit Stones E and F / Amunra Stone | Explains class E and F spirit stones, which grant piercing damage and damage absorption, with class F composed at the NPC Shany by Arch 356+ characters. It also covers adding base stats with Ankhs of Justice, Eternity and Glory at Ehre in Erion, and notes that Amunra stones follow the same rules. |
| [Refinação de Essence of Gods](essence-of-gods.md) | Essence of Gods Refinement | Covers refining the Essence of Gods at the NPC Conjurer Ehre in Erion with Lac 100 dust, Lac dust, a Bless Stabilizer and 1kk gold. Only celestial characters of level 320 or higher can attempt it, and failure destroys everything except the Essence. |
| [Capas](capes.md) | Capes | Guide to kingdom capes, which set a character's kingdom: mortals get a red or blue cape through the Kingdom Knight quest at level 220 or by bringing sapphires to the King above level 250. It also covers switching with the Peerage Lift, cape borders at levels 255 and 355, and capes for Arch characters. |
| [Fadas](fairies.md) | Fairies | Guide to the five fairies (blue, green, red, silver and golden), support items that boost drop rate and experience. Blue, green and red are sold by Premium Neil, while silver and golden are made from a red fairy with the Silver Flash from Farche in Erion or the Golden Flash from Mai, and they auto-sell non-stackable drops. |
| [Fragmentos da Alma](soul-fragments.md) | Soul Fragments | Soul Fragments are traded in sets of 12 to the NPC Alchemy Annie in Azran for a random Phoenix or Unicorn Soul, used to make the Stone of Immortality for Arch, Celestial and Sub Celestial characters. They drop from RujeperBroken in the desert, LichVatama and LichCrunt in Kefra, and the Unknown Desert. |
| [Pergaminhos da Água](water-scrolls.md) | Water Scrolls | Water Scrolls open timed experience rooms in the water cave near Azran for groups of at least 8 players. The Normal and Mystic scrolls are for mortals and Archs, while the Arcane scroll is only for celestials. |
| [Premium Neil (Prisang Neil)](premium-neil.md) | Premium Neil (Prisang Neil) | Premium Neil is the NPC found in every city next to the Cargo Guard who sells all NP (cash) items, organized in tabs named after the game's cities. Listed items include fairies, experience chests, name-change stones, Divine Potions, sapphires, Replation and jewels for Ancient items. |

# Progressão

*Progression.* Evolução do personagem: Mortal, Arch, Celestial, Sub-Celestial.

| Página | English | Resumo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Guia para Novatos](beginners-guide.md) | Beginner's Guide | A beginner's guide to WYD Global covering the green newbie channel, setting a numeric password, picking one of the four classes and the starting Training Field quest. In that quest, players talk to the Unicornio and CabuncleWyd NPCs and defeat the Orc boss for the Orc emblem. It also explains the interface and hotkeys. |
| [Arch](arch.md) | Arch | Covers the Arch class, unlocked after completing the quests A procura da vida eterna and Um novo começo, which creates a second character with the same name. It explains the Arch's equipment restrictions (no helmets, class E weapons or Lydians), the bonuses gained at level 370 and per-level stat growth by class. |
| [Criação do Celestial](celestial-creation.md) | Celestial Creation | Explains how to create a Celestial, which needs a level 400 mortal, an Arch of at least level 356, the four secret stones, the class Saphirot and the Pedra da Imortalidade. Odin in Kefra handles the secret stones, and Ehre in Erion configures the Limite da Alma (Soul) skill using jewel combinations. |
| [Desbloqueio do Level 40 - Celestial](celestial-level-40-unlock.md) | Celestial Level 40 Unlock | Explains how to unlock a Celestial's level cap at 40 by delivering the runes Fehu, Mannaz, Thuirisaz and Ansuz in that order, plus 2 sealed scrolls and a magic seal, to the NPC Odin. |
| [Desbloqueio do Level 90 - Celestial](celestial-level-90-unlock.md) | Celestial Level 90 Unlock | Explains how to unlock a Celestial's level cap at 90. The runes Sowilo, Kenaz, Uraz, Thurisaz, Isa, Tiwaz and Hagalaz go to the NPC Odin in order, with 2,000,000 gold, to create the Pedra da Fúria, which can fail when used. |
| [Reset Celestial Level 200](celestial-reset-level-200.md) | Celestial Level 200 Reset | After creating the Cythera Arcana at level 200, Celestials and Sub-Celestials can reset up to 3 times for 160 points each. Each reset needs level 200+ (4 billion experience), 1000 fame and a Pedra da Fúria, and it consumes 50 million experience. |
| [Desbloqueio Nível 240 a 360 - Celestial](celestial-level-240-360-unlock.md) | Celestial Level 240 to 360 Unlock | Explains unlocking a Celestial's levels 240, 280, 320 and 360 with the Proteção Divina item, composed at the NPC Dedekinto in Kefra from 2 Coração Beriel Amald and 2 Alma Beriel (25% success). The level 240 unlock never fails, while from 280 both characters must be the same level and there is a 50% failure chance. |
| [Sub Celestial](sub-celestial.md) | Sub-Celestial | Explains creating a Sub-Celestial from a Celestial of level 120+ using 100 fame, a Sephirot of the chosen class and a Pedra Ideal, then switching between the two with the Pedra Misteriosa made at the NPC Ehre in Erion. It covers the Cythera bonuses the main character gets from the Sub's level and notes that the Sub must relearn all Sephira skills. |
| [Cythera Arcana](cythera.md) | Cythera Arcana | Explains creating the Cythera Arcana (A), the highest tiara tier, with a level 200+ Celestial or Sub-Celestial, the four secret stones, a Pedra da Fúria, a Cythera M +11 and 1000 fame, at a 50% success rate. It also covers recreating it at the NPC Arcane Jeweler in Kefra from level 241, which takes 2 of each secret stone, 2 Pedras da Fúria, 4 Valkyria emblems and 100kk gold. |
